# Space Riders: Cosmic Board

**Play free in your browser:** https://sanyziahj.github.io/space-riders-board/

A neon sci-fi hoverboard trick game. The boards really hover: no wheels, a glowing underglow, thruster flames, and magnetic beams for grinding rails. It plays like SSX Tricky meets THPS3 meets T&C Surf Designs. Ride side-scrolling courses, and the camera swings behind the rider for a forward pseudo-3D view at corners and big drops. Chain tricks, grabs and grinds into combos. Blast Void icons and drones with your raygun. Fill the boost meter and fire your rider's signature **Uber** trick. Grab the letters **L-E-T-S R-I-D-E** in every stage.

**Sky lane:** every stage has an upper route. Hold UP + BOOST to rise into it. It has its own hazards (cranes, dust storms, neon signs, comets, Void tentacles, blimp cables and searchlights), extra drones, bonus rings, high-value targets and letters you can only get up there. Watch your ALT (hover energy) bar.

The Drill Sergeant runs the SpaceRider Academy. He grumbles about flashy tricks, but he LOVES a good blast: *"NOW THAT'S A BOOM!"* Push forward!

## Stages
1. SpaceRider Academy: the Drill Sergeant hands you his DS Cadet Deck, then 12 gated drill stations (one skill each) and a graduation run. Once you pass, you can skip straight to the graduation run.
2. Space Yards
3. Pioneer Pour
4. Andromeda Casino
5. Cosmic Waves
6. Hi-Life Bar (the finale aboard the silver neon blimp)

## Riders
Jazz, Rihcter, Skippy and Cavendish, each with their own stats, raygun and Uber. Clear the finale to unlock a hidden rider.

## Unlocks
- Medals open new stages, boards (Cadet Deck, Rail Phantom, Interceptor, Nebula Carver) and paint jobs.
- Gold on every stage unlocks the Cosmic Hoverboard.
- Clearing the Hi-Life Bar unlocks the Victory Poster download.
- Progress saves in your browser. Pick NEW GAME any time to start fresh.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steer / speed / tuck | Arrows or WASD | D-pad / left stick | Stick |
| Jump (hold for higher) | Space / J | A | JUMP |
| Trick (+direction for flips) | K | X | TRICK |
| Grab | L | B | GRAB |
| Blast | I / F | Y / RB | BLAST |
| Boost | Shift / O | LT / RT | BOOST |
| Uber (full meter) | U | LB | UBER |
| Rise to the sky lane | Up + Shift | D-pad up + RT | Stick up + BOOST |
| Drop from the sky lane | Down | D-pad down | Stick down |
| Pause | Enter (Esc in a run) | Start | START |
| Menus: OK / Back | Space or Enter / Esc | A / B | JUMP / GRAB |

M mutes the sound. Every on-screen prompt names the real button for the device you used last (keyboard, gamepad or touch), e.g. "PRESS SPACE TO JUMP" or "PRESS A TO JUMP". Let's Ride!

Single-file HTML5 canvas game with procedural WebAudio music. All ages.
